Failed deliveries, fixed in the customer's own language.

MileMend helps online sellers in India recover orders that could not be delivered. It messages the customer on WhatsApp, SMS or email in Gujarati, Hindi or English, then updates the delivery plan with their answer.

How it works

  1. The seller or courier reports a problem. MileMend receives a delivery event, for example a failed attempt.
  2. The customer gets a message. It arrives on WhatsApp first, then SMS or email if there is no reply, in the customer's language.
  3. The reply is turned into an action. An AI assistant understands the answer and picks an approved action, such as a new delivery time or a corrected address.
  4. The seller stays in control. Higher-value or unclear cases go to a manager for approval, and every action is recorded.

What MileMend is today

MileMend is a proof of concept built on AWS. It is tested with consenting test users and sample data.

Consent and privacy

MileMend messages only people who have agreed to receive delivery updates. A customer can reply STOP at any time to stop messages. Phone numbers are stored encrypted and are used only to resolve the delivery.
